FAQ

Questions we get asked

If yours is not here, call or message us — we would rather answer it directly.

What equipment do you manufacture?
Poultry ventilation fans (box, push-pull, cone and 36" circulation), cooling pad systems, automatic pan feeding lines, feed silos and hoppers, nipple drinking systems, automatic control panels, gas brooders, insulation, rubber cage matting, tunnel doors and air inlet systems.
Are you certified?
Yes. Our quality management system is certified to ISO 9001:2015 under certificate UC-01559, covering ventilation, feeding, drinking, brooding, cooling and environmental control systems. We are also Udyam (MSME) registered and recognised as a startup by DPIIT.
How do I get a quote?
Send your shed dimensions, bird count and location through the contact form, by WhatsApp, or by phone. We come back with a system layout and a price.
Do you export?
Our company objects cover importing, exporting and distributing poultry equipment, and we welcome overseas enquiries. Tell us your country and nearest port and we will quote.
How many birds can feed from one pan?
Eighteen birds can feed from one pan at a time. The pan is designed so birds can eat from day one, and the design restricts birds from entering the pan.
What size is your feed silo?
Our feed silo has a 14 ton capacity. The godown hopper stores roughly 1 to 1.5 ton for smaller bulk requirements.
Can the control panel work with equipment I already have?
Yes. The automatic control panel is built to integrate with all types of shed equipment, new or old, and supports a full array of sensors.
Do you install and support the equipment?
Customer support covering installation, maintenance and troubleshooting is one of the objects the company was incorporated to provide. Talk to us about what your site needs.
Where are you located?
Our works and sales office is at Near Hotel Ananta, Panipat Road, Safidon, Jind, Haryana 126112, India. We have a second unit at Todi Khedi Mod, Safidon.
