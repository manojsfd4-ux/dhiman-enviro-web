Manufacturing

Built in our own works, not bought in

Fans, cabinets, inlets and feed hardware are fabricated, assembled and finished on site at Safidon.

On the shop floor

What happens here

01

Sheet metal cutting

Shearing galvanised sheet to size for fan housings, cabinets and inlet frames.

02

Press work

Power press forming of fan blades and pressed components in batch runs.

03

Fabrication

Housing frames, guards and duct sections built up from cut and formed stock.

04

Fan assembly

Impellers balanced and mounted, motors fitted, guards and louvres installed.

05

Finishing

Galvanised and coated surfaces prepared for the corrosive shed environment.

06

Packing & dispatch

Units wrapped and staged in the yard for road transport to site.
