How the shed breathes

Air in, over the birds, out.

Cooling pads draw fresh air in at one end. It travels the length of the house over the birds and is pulled out by exhaust fans at the other — all governed by one control panel.

01 — Air in

Cooling pad system

Evaporative pads at the inlet end cool and filter incoming air.

02 — Governed by

Control panel

Sensors and staged fans hold temperature and airflow automatically.

03 — Air out

Exhaust fans

High-throughput fans pull stale, warm air out at the far end.
