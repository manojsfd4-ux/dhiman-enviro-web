How the shed breathes

Air in, over the birds, out.

Cooling pads draw fresh air in at one end. It travels the length of the house over the birds and is pulled out by exhaust fans at the other — all governed by one control panel.

Cooling pad · cool air inover the birdsExhaust fans · warm air out
01 — Air in

Cooling pad system

Evaporative pads at the inlet end cool and filter incoming air.

02 — Governed by

Control panel

Sensors and staged fans hold temperature and airflow automatically.

03 — Air out

Exhaust fans

High-throughput fans pull stale, warm air out at the far end.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do you install as well as supply?

Yes. Our team installs and commissions the system on your farm, and trains your staff to run and maintain it.

How do I get a price?

Send us the shed dimensions, bird count and location. We come back with a system layout and a price. Call, email or WhatsApp — whichever is easier.

Can I buy just one system?

Yes. Each system works on its own. You can buy a single feeding line or fan bank, or let us specify the whole house.

Do you supply outside Haryana?

Yes — across India. Tell us the destination and we work out packing and delivery.
